## QABC-0025 | A36:D36 | G1-ELE-029 / G2-ELEC-178
> Curentul electric alternativ poate fi produs numai prin fenomene: A termice; B chimice; C de inducţie electromagnetică
- **C is the conventional alternator-generation intended candidate** if asking specifically about a rotating electrical generator using induction; A and B alone do not describe the usual direct generation of sinusoidal AC in that traditional setup.
- **Absolute `numai` is contradicted by valid counterexample:** U.S. Department of Energy explicitly describes semiconductor inverters electronically switching a DC supply to create AC, including sinusoidal output after filtering; modern grid-forming inverters can produce AC without a rotating induction generator. Source: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-inverters-and-grid-services-basics .
- Thus do NOT approve an unconditional claim that all AC can only be generated by electromagnetic induction. The original stem **does not specify `generator electromecanic`** and cannot silently acquire that condition. This does **not** mean A or B become correct replacements.
- **Disposition:** `CONVENTIONAL_C_CANDIDATE / UNIVERSAL_QUANTIFIER_COUNTEREXAMPLE / SOURCE_ERA_REVIEW / NOT_APPROVED`.

## QABC-0042 | A56:D56 | G1-ELE-049 / G2-ELEC-195
> Dacă două conductoare parcurse de curenţi în acelaşi sens sunt aşezate paralel, unul lângă altul: A densitatea de curent crește în părţile apropiate ...; B densitatea de curent creşte în părţile mai depărtate ...; C densitatea de curent este uniformă ...
- **B is the technically supported proximity-effect candidate FOR AC**: same-direction neighboring AC conductors have higher current density on the sides farthest from the other conductor, relative to the nearby facing sides. A reverses that direction; C ignores the nonuniform effect.
- Crucial context missing from original text: **AC versus DC and frequency**, conductor geometry and regime. In simple homogeneous steady-DC conductors, the time-varying magnetic field that drives ordinary AC proximity effect is absent; the claimed redistribution is not universal for unspecified `curenţi`. Note also skin effect and proximity effect must not be conflated.
- Sources: https://piexpertonline.power.com/help/piexpert/en/popups/proximity_effect.htm ; https://technav.ieee.org/topic/proximity-effect/ .
- **Disposition:** `AC_CONDITIONAL_B_CANDIDATE / MISSING_AC_OR_FREQUENCY_SCOPE / NOT_APPROVED`.

## QABC-0069 | A84:D84 | G1-ELE-077 / G2-ELEC-222
> Forţa care se exercită asupra unui conductor rectiliniu, parcurs de curentul i, aflat în câmpul de inducţie magnetică B se numeşte: A forţă electromagnetică (Laplace); B forţă electrodinamică; C forţă magnetomotoare
- **A directly and specifically matches** standard named Laplace force on a current-carrying conductor in an external magnetic field, with `dF=I dl×B` or `F=B I l sin(theta)` for a straight length in uniform field. Politecnico di Milano independent published research explicitly calls it Laplace force: https://re.public.polimi.it/handle/11311/1233539 .
- A Romanian didactic source distinguishes **electromagnetic force on a conductor in a field** from **electrodynamic force between two nearby current-carrying conductors**: https://www.fizichim.ro/docs/fizica/clasa10/capitolul2-producerea-si-utilizarea-curentului-continuu/II-15-efectele-curentului-electric/II-15-2-efectul-magnetic-al-curentului-electric-aplicatiile-efectului-magnetic/ .
- B may be used as a broader engineering force adjective in some contexts; for *this* original stem, the explicitly named Laplace concept is more specific. C concerns magnetomotive-force/ampere-turn, not Newtonian mechanical force on the wire.
- **Disposition:** `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_A_CANDIDATE / TERMINOLOGY_AND_EDITION_PENDING / NOT_APPROVED`.

## QABC-0091 | A106:D106 | G1-ELE-099 / G2-ELEC-242

- **Disposition:** `CONTROL_SCHEME_MISSING / NO_UNIQUE_SOURCE_AUTHENTICATED_KEY / NOT_APPROVED`.
