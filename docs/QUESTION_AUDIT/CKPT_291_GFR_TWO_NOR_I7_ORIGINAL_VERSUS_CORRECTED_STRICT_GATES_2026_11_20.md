## QABC-0362 / G1-NOR-048, 2023 NOR row 55: bell/doorbell supply

No exception reversing this list found in immediate context.
- **Stem↔A:** false **`numai`**, as normal lighting and sockets are also permitted. **Stem↔B:** false **`numai`**, as distribution board and sockets also permitted. **Stem↔C:** directly affirmed **permission** to supply from socket circuit, not an obligation to do so.

Această conectare trebuie să fie sigură și deconectabilă numai prin intermediul unei scule.` Directly neighboring **5.5.2.2** concerns when each PE is or is not connected DIRECTLY to main bar via other principal PE paths, not an exemption from individually disconnecting conductors **that are connected**.
