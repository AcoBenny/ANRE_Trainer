## Dated, qualified findings
1. **ANRE live page (crawled 21 November 2026)** explicitly still lists `Tematica si bibliografie pentru examenul de autorizare electricieni 2023` with published question workbook links dated 2023/2024; page also mentions `Sesiunii TOAMNĂ 2026`. This supports continued public availability of the **2023 bibliography** in the 2026 official portal, but a static list alone does NOT prove that every cited 2023 provision applies unamended to 2026 exam scoring: https://anre.ro/participanti-la-piata-de-energie/persoane-fizice/energie-electrica/
2. **I7 foundational approval** is Ordin nr. **2741/2011**: https://lexapp.ro/legislatie/ordinul-2741-2011-ministerul-dezvoltarii-regionale-turismului/ . A specific published amendment exists: **Ordin nr. 959/2023**: https://lexapp.ro/legislatie/ordinul-959-2023-ministerul-dezvoltarii-lucrarilor-publice/ . This corroborates the 2011/2023 version history explicitly noted on user's I7 PDF, not that the specific uploaded copy reproduces all changes correctly through November 2026.
3. A different **September 2026 public exam bibliography**, at the Court of Appeal portal (for judicial technical expert, **NOT an ANRE electrician examination**), explicitly references I7-2011 with changes under Ordin 959/2023. https://portal.just.ro/46/Documents/5.Tematic%C4%83%20examen%20Instala%C8%9Bii%20pentru%20construc%C8%9Bii%20-%2023.09.2026.pdf . This indicates modern institutional continued recognition of the normative pairing, but its different exam scope prevents treating it as ANRE Grade I/II edition certification.
4. Secondary updated October 31, 2026 technical digest also describes I7-2011 as modified under 959/2023: https://www.dioda.ro/blog/normativ-i7-text-integral . Not primary legal authority.
5. Targeted web searches did **not surface** a later specific I7 amendment, but an unsuccessful search **cannot prove none exists**. There was no complete authoritative 2026 Portal Legislativ consolidated-article audit, later amendments registry proof or 2026 ANRE exam-key authentication here.
