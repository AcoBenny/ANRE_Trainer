## Focused findings, first priority
1. `G1-ELE-028 / G2-ELEC-177`: **explicit missing diagram** 'schema de mai sus'. B increasing reactance reduces fault current in an abstract scheme but cannot be declared *preferred* for unseen topology. `BLOCKED_MISSING_AUTHENTIC_FIGURE`.
2. `G1-ELE-043 / G2-ELEC-189`: alleged linear relation of stator current and voltage is not universal; from balanced 3ph `I=|S|/(sqrt(3)U)`, for fixed S actually inverse in U. None of A/B/C unconditionally true without operating assumptions. `NO_UNIVERSAL_OPTION_PROVEN`.
3. `G1-ELE-073 / G2-ELEC-218`: ambiguity electric flux `E` vs electric displacement `D`. Gauss `int(E·dA)=q/epsilon0`, while `int(D·dA)=q_free`. Imported B (=q) fits *D flux* not bare E-flux in SI; source [OpenStax §6.2](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/6-2-explaining-gausss-law). `DEFINITION_GATE`.
4. `G1-ELE-104 / G2-ELEC-247`: imported A says oil **'doar' insulates**, yet oil is arc-quenching dielectric in oil circuit breakers, which makes B contextually valid in this equipment class. C is mechanical lubrication, not the asked electrical function. Need equipment class. The general [IEC 60422](https://www.iec.ch/system/files/2024-02/iec_60422_-_1_feb_2024_-_v2e.pdf) covers mineral oil in multiple kinds of electrical equipment. An additional separate AI research model run produced a corroborating analysis, **not a professional QA reviewer** (research response id resp_e669a211-e777-4426-ba0d-6f0533be3377; no claim this is a certified independent review).
5. `G1-ELE-128 / G2-ELEC-268`: imported A 'voltage before current by 90 degrees'; correct only for **ideal pure L**, not a general inductive-character RL where `phi=atan(omega L/R)`, `0<phi<90°` for R>0. B and C reject by sign/direction, but A only textbook-intended model. [MIT OCW AC circuits](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-spring-2007/e7fd966c84001f4e8fc0f58a6751013e_cha12ac_circuits.pdf). Separate AI research also flagged pure-L assumption (response id resp_458a4eee-e69f-4652-bdfc-655eebaca709), still **NOT independent QA**.
6. `G1-ELE-222 / G2-ELEC-358`: susceptance in general `B=Im(1/(R+jX))=-X/(R²+X²)`; not `1/X` except special pure-reactance magnitude with sign caveat; imported C conventional oversimplification. [All About Circuits](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/alternating-current/chpt-5/susceptance-and-admittance/).
7. `G1-ELE-245 / G2-ELEC-381`: power factor 1 establishes unity net displacement factor, but a series RLC at resonance may have it although not made solely of resistors. Imported A 'pur rezistiv' is best of options if describing **equivalent** impedance, not guaranteed component composition. [OpenStax AC RLC](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/15-3-rlc-series-circuits-with-ac).
8. `G1-ELE-029 / G2-ELEC-178`: 'numai prin inducţie electromagnetică' applies conventional rotating generators, but DC-to-AC semiconductor inverters produce AC; [US DOE inverter basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-inverters-and-grid-services-basics).
9. `G1-ELE-099 / G2-ELEC-242`: spontaneous restart depends on contactor/no-voltage protection/restart policy, not just rotor architecture; requires electrical controls. `A_AUTORESTART_CONDITIONAL`.
10. `G1-ELE-014`: 'câmp fără surse' requires original mathematical field-definition; `ELE-049` proximity-current distribution requires explicit frequency and geometry; `ELE-057` definition of harmonic, same-frequency and reciprocal-period cases overlap; `ELE-077` 'Laplace' versus broad electrodynamic terminology; `ELE-155` deep-bar and double-cage starting distinction; `ELE-178` reactive power explanation versus instantaneous dW/dt.
